Welcome to Paws & Hope Animal Shelter

Paws & Hope is a community-based animal shelter dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming stray and abandoned animals in our local area. Every animal that comes through our doors receives medical care, food, and shelter while we search for a safe, loving, permanent home.

A Look Inside Our Shelter

Every day, our shelter is home to dozens of animals waiting for their forever families. From playful pups to gentle seniors, each one receives food, medical care, and plenty of attention while they wait to be adopted, fostered, or sponsored.
