Our Programmes

Paws & Hope offers a range of programmes designed to help animals in need and to make it easy for the community to get involved. Browse our programmes below and use the enquiry form to get started.

Adoption

Our adoption programme matches rescued animals with loving, permanent homes. All animals are vaccinated, sterilised, and health-checked before adoption. Adoption fees help cover the cost of this care.

  • Meet-and-greet visits available by appointment.
  • Home-suitability discussion with our adoption coordinator.
  • Follow-up support after adoption.

Fostering

Foster carers provide temporary homes for animals that are too young, unwell, or anxious to cope in the shelter environment. Paws & Hope supplies food, bedding, and veterinary support to all approved foster carers.

  • Flexible, short- and long-term fostering options.
  • Ideal for those not ready for a permanent pet.
  • Training and support provided before placement.

Sponsorship

Sponsors provide vital financial support that covers food, medical treatment, and shelter upkeep. Individuals, families, and businesses can all become sponsors.

  • Monthly or once-off sponsorship options.
  • Corporate sponsorship packages with recognition on our site.
  • Sponsor a specific animal or the shelter as a whole.
